GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

What types of requests can I send?

You can enquire about business computers, servers, printers, networking, IT support, surveillance, access control, security and other technology services.

Can QBranch provide a quotation?

Use the enquiry form or contact the sales email to describe the products, quantity, scope or technical needs so the team can discuss an appropriate quotation.

Does the website accept online payments?

No. This is an information and enquiry website. It does not process online payments or collect card details.

Will the online form send my enquiry automatically?

Not yet. Because this website is hosted statically on GitHub Pages, it prepares an email in your own email application. You must press Send there. You can also email sales@qbranch.co.ug directly.

Where is the QBranch office?

The existing company website lists the Uganda Cooperative Alliance Building, Plot 47/49, Nkrumah Road, Kampala, Uganda.
