Contacting QBranch

When you use the enquiry form, your browser prepares an email containing the details you entered. You decide whether to send it using your email service. This website itself does not upload, retain or process the form contents on a server. You can alternatively call or email QBranch directly.

Information sent by email

If you send an email to a QBranch address, it is delivered through your email provider and handled through the recipient’s email systems. Please avoid sharing passwords, banking credentials or sensitive personal information in ordinary enquiry emails.

Third-party resources

The website may request fonts from Google Fonts and illustrative photos from Unsplash. External map links open Google Maps. These providers may process connection information under their own policies.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not intentionally set tracking cookies and does not include an analytics tracker at launch.

For questions about the website or enquiries, contact info@qbranch.co.ug or use the information on the Contact page.
