OUR COMPANY

Bringing Life
to Technology.

QBranch Uganda Limited is an ICT services, general supplies and consultancy business based in Kampala, Uganda.

Our existing company profile describes a team supporting corporations, non-governmental organisations and public institutions with access to IT products and solutions. Our areas span networking, computing equipment, secure infrastructure, surveillance, access control, managed services and business technology.

We believe the best solutions start with understanding how an organisation operates — then connecting it with technology suited to its environment.
